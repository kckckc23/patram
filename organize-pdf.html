Organize PDF pages online, free and private

Reorder, rotate, or drop pages, then rebuild the PDF. Use Patram to organize pdf pages online for free in your browser — no upload and no account — so you can handle this task privately on your own device.

Everything happens locally in your browser: your files are processed on-device via WebAssembly and are never uploaded to Patram’s servers. No account or signup is required, and your documents stay private on your own machine. This tool runs on your device using Python compiled to WebAssembly (Pyodide). The engine loads on demand and is cached after the first download so it can work offline on later visits. Open the tool when you are ready, choose your files or input, review the options, and save the finished result back to your device.

How to use Organize & rotate

  1. Open Organize & rotate in Patram and add the file or content you want to work with.
  2. Adjust the available options for organize & rotate, then start processing on your device.
  3. Review the result and download the finished output — your files never leave this device.
