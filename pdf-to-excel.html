PDF → Excel

Detect real tables into an .xlsx workbook, sheet per page. Patram’s PDF → Excel tool helps you handle this task directly in your browser without sending documents anywhere else. It is designed for quick, private document work where you keep full control over your files from start to finish.

Everything happens locally in your browser: your files are processed on-device via WebAssembly and are never uploaded to Patram’s servers. No account or signup is required, and your documents stay private on your own machine. Because this conversion uses the table-detection engine (pdfplumber), expect a one-time engine download of about 8 MB on first use before processing begins. The first time you use this tool, Patram downloads its table-detection engine (pdfplumber) (about 8 MB) from a CDN; after that, the engine is cached for offline use. Open the tool when you are ready, choose your files or input, review the options, and save the finished result back to your device.

How to use PDF → Excel

  1. Open PDF → Excel in Patram and add the file or content you want to work with.
  2. Adjust the available options for pdf → excel, then start processing on your device.
  3. Review the result and download the finished output — your files never leave this device.
