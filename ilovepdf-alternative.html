A free iLovePDF alternative that never uploads your files

Patram offers the same kinds of everyday PDF tools — merge, split, compress, convert and OCR — with one key difference: where your files go. With Patram, documents are processed on your device in your browser instead of being sent to a server for processing.

Comparison

Feature iLovePDF Patram
Where files are processed Files are uploaded to iLovePDF's servers for processing On your device, in your browser (WebAssembly)
Account needed Not required for basic use No

File-size limits Plan-based limits Limited by your device’s available memory
Works offline No Yes, after the first load and engine downloads
Open source No Yes (AGPL-3.0)

When Patram is a better fit

  • Sensitive documents: useful for contracts, IDs, financial records, or anything you prefer to keep on your own device.
  • Slow or metered connections: once the app and required engines are cached, you can work without sending files over the network.
  • Offline work: after the first load, tools can run without an internet connection.

When iLovePDF might fit better

  • You want dedicated mobile apps.
  • You want an integrated e-signing workflow.
